WHAT IT DOES

On the paper
  - The first print looks the same as today.
  - Every print after that has a sheet number on the end of the receipt
    number:  No. RCP-482913-A/2,  /3,  /4 ...
  - One small bold line at the bottom: the date and time of printing, and
    who printed it.

On the Transactions page
  - Headings fit on one line, and rows no longer wrap.
  - The Receipt button shows how many times it has been printed:
      Receipt ×1   grey
      Receipt ×3   gold (printed more than once)
      Receipt      never printed since this went live
  - Click the ×3 to see each print: sheet, date and time, and who.

On the receipt screen, before pressing Print
  - A strip on top says "Already printed 3 times" with each time and name.
    It never prints.

Works offline: a station with no signal still records its prints, and
they are sent with the next sync, at the real time they were printed.


WHAT IT COUNTS

It counts every press of Print. If someone presses Print and then cancels
in the printer box, that still counts. The browser cannot tell whether
paper actually came out.

Tickets printed before this goes live show no ×, because nothing was
recorded back then.


CHECK AFTER UPLOADING
  1. Open any ticket's Receipt and press Print.
  2. Go back. The Receipt button now shows ×1.
  3. Press Print again. The paper says .../2 and the button shows ×2
     in gold.
  4. Click ×2. Both prints are listed with time and name.
