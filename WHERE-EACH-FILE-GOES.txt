WHAT IT DOES

  An amber box at the top of the HQ Dashboard:
    "Ping Pong's scale has sent nothing for 47 minutes"

  It only appears when the scale WAS working today and has stopped:
    - under 15 minutes quiet  -> nothing (that is a reboot or a slow minute)
    - over 6 hours quiet      -> nothing (the station closed for the night)
    - never sent anything ever -> nothing (no agent installed there)

  An alarm that goes off on a normal evening is an alarm everyone learns to
  ignore, so it deliberately stays silent in all three of those cases.
